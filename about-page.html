OUR STORY

The VELORA Philosopy

VELORA was founded on a simple conviction: a woman needs fewer, better clothes. We design a tight collection of essential pieces a perfect coat, a dress that works day to evening, a shirt you reach for every week.


Every garment starts with the fabric. We source natural wool, organic cotton and washed linen, then cut each piece simply and finish it by hand in our atelier. Nothing is rushed, nothing is excessive  fashion that whispers, never shouts.
